The question

A market may produce excellent returns in its local currency. But what return does a foreign investor actually keep once the money is converted back home?

A practical example

If you earn and save in euros in Germany or elsewhere in Europe, your investment in India follows this journey:

€ → ₹ → NIFTY 50 → ₹ → €

A weaker rupee against the euro reduces your return; a stronger rupee can help. What matters is the wealth you bring home.
